UCSB Summer 2025 and 2026

Online, in-person and hybrid regular instructional offerings

Historical schedule data retrieved October 18, 2026. Both quarters had ended. UCSB does not identify an enrollment freeze date on the public search, so these are post-quarter displayed enrollments, not certified end-of-quarter census counts.

Online enrollment share rose from 43.9% to 55.9%. Online class counts rose from 198 to 240, while in-person class counts fell from 476 to 388. Total included course enrollments were nearly unchanged: 25,134 versus 25,168.

The main comparison covers positive-enrollment regular offerings, consolidates four cross-list groups, and counts a lecture with its associated discussions once. Hybrid and unknown delivery remain separate. Scheduled zero-enrollment listings are shown below as a sensitivity.

Methodology and sources

Scope

Summer 2025 and Summer 2026, every one of the 98 subject options, Course Level = All (undergraduate and graduate). Retrieved October 18, 2026. Main results cover regular instructional offerings with positive displayed enrollment.

Enrollment date

Both summers had ended by retrieval. The public search does not provide an enrollment census/freeze date or certify that the displayed counts equal the last day of the quarter. These are post-quarter displayed historical enrollments, not independently verified official end-of-quarter census counts. The page warns of up to 24 hours of delay.

Counting unit

One primary instructional offering per summer session and section, not one distinct catalog course number. A standalone credit-bearing laboratory is a class. Primary lecture enrollment is counted once; discussion, lab, and other subordinate rows are retained as evidence but never added to course enrollments or class counts. Different sessions and parallel sections remain distinct.

Enrollment meaning

Course enrollments are registrations, not unique students. A student taking two courses contributes two enrollments. Use the numerator in displayed Enrolled / Capacity; capacity is never substituted for enrollment.

Cross-listings

Four groups were consolidated using matching description, instructor, session, time and location: 2025 C LIT 154I / FR 154I; 2025 C LIT 171 / FR 154G; 2026 C LIT 50C / FR 50CX; 2026 C LIT 187 / GER 187 / SLAV 187. Count one shared class and sum registrations across its listings. All these groups fall in Humanities & Fine Arts. Identical course numbers with different sections are not merged.

Exclusions

Exclude 98, 99, 198, 199 and suffixed variants; directed reading, independent research, thesis/dissertation preparation (including 596–599); explicit canceled records; zero-enrollment arranged placeholders. Individual internship and student-teaching placements are outside regular classroom instruction and excluded. Research-methods classes and ordinary seminars are retained.

Zero-enrollment sensitivity

A further 9 scheduled zero-enrollment listings in 2025 and 13 in 2026 are held outside main results, with their own decision label. Most are closed lab sections; four across the two years are INT 90M research-skills seminars and one is MCDB 1AZ. Their actual delivery cannot be verified. Adding these listings changes class counts/means only; enrollment totals stay the same. Do not interpret Closed as Canceled.

Online

ON LINE and ON ASYNC are online, consistent with UCSB Summer Sessions guidance. A mix of synchronous and asynchronous components is still online when all identified components are online. The W in a course ID alone is not used to classify delivery.

Hybrid

Hybrid means the public schedule shows both an online component and an identified physical location in the same primary/associated section group. All positive-enrollment subordinate components are considered. This is an operational schedule-based category, not a claim about the university official modality code. Unknown/TBA components cannot establish hybrid status.

In person and unknown

In person requires an identified physical primary/associated meeting and no identified online component. Unknown / arranged retains otherwise genuine instruction for which the public schedule does not establish delivery. Four clinical/field practicums per year and EEMB 194M in 2026 remain unknown. Unknown delivery is not automatically in person.

Small enrollments

Every primary record with 1–3 enrollments was individually reviewed using the title, description, scheduled meeting and associated components. Small genuine lectures, labs, seminars and supervised clinic instruction remain. Internship, research and dissertation exclusions are based on course type, not size. FR 50CX with two registrations is retained within its shared nine-student class. The repeated CHEM CS 199 record is excluded independent study and documented as a duplicate.

Divisions

Letters & Science uses Humanities & Fine Arts; Mathematical, Life & Physical Sciences; and Social Sciences. Psychology, Geography and Environmental Studies belong to MLPS; History belongs to HFA. Education (ED and CNCSP), Engineering (including TMP), Creative Studies and Bren School are separate reporting units. INT / GRAD are reported as Interdisciplinary / Other rather than forced into a disciplinary division. Subject-to-division mapping is included in the evidence archive.

Percentages and means

Class share = modality classes / all included classes in the same year and division. Enrollment share = modality course enrollments / all included course enrollments in the same year and division. Average class size = modality enrollments / modality classes. Hybrid and unknown remain in percentage denominators. Empty categories have zero counts and an unavailable mean. Percentages may not sum to 100.0% after rounding.

Audit trail

Courses contains every primary record, decision, class-count and enrollment contribution, cross-list identifier, tiny-enrollment review and snapshot reference. Source rows preserves all 3,427 displayed rows and primary/secondary links. Stable record IDs are year-subject-option-index-row. Source row numbers are zero-based among direct result-table rows. Four cross-list groups reduce included primary listings by two in 2025 and three in 2026.

Interpretation limit

This is a descriptive comparison of registrations and listed class sections. It does not measure unique students, student credit hours, instructional effort, learning outcomes, or a causal effect of online delivery. Changes in subject/session mix and class size affect the aggregate shares.
